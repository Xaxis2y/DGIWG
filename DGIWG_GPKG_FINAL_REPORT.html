ℹ️ Scope of these verdicts
Every verdict in this report covers the 34 requirements this tool can test automatically. Req 1 and Req 2 (OGC base conformance) require the OGC CITE TeamEngine test suites, and Req 6 (conditional extensions) depends on the applicable DGIWG product profile — all three are reported as SKIPPED and must be verified separately before a deliverable is declared fully conformant to STD-DP-19-005 v1.1.
How to read this table: Validator Disputes = how many GPKGs got FAIL from this script's automated checks. Cross-Checks & Diagnosis = probable root cause for each FAIL/PASS* result (by source software), plus a collapsible auditor cross-check panel with known blind spots and caveats. PASS* & Manual Verification = automated PASS* confirmation count, plus expandable manual inspection notes. Confidence: High = fully automated and confirmed; Medium = partial check or single known gap; Low = known validator bug or cannot be automated.
